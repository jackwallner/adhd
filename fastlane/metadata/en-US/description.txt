A clearer next step for the routines that shape your day.

Next Cue is a routine coach and daily planner for people who want a little more structure. Turn a big intention into small, visible steps. Choose when to begin, keep the next action in view, and use gentle reminders to return when your attention moves elsewhere.

MAKE A ROUTINE
• Create morning, evening, or custom routines.
• Break a routine into simple, manageable steps.
• Add, remove, and edit steps as your day changes.

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT
• Keep the next action easy to find.
• Mark steps complete and see how the routine is going.
• Get reminders at the times you choose.

MAKE ROOM FOR REAL LIFE
• Adjust a plan without starting over.
• Pick up again when your day changes.
• Begin again without streaks or guilt.
