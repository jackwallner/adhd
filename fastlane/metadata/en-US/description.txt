For the moment you know what to do and still can't start.

Next Cue breaks your routines into small steps and helps you begin the one in front of you. When a step feels too big, tap I'm stuck and it shrinks to a single tiny move: put on one sock, fill the glass, open one window. Once you've started, the whole step comes back and you keep going.

WHEN YOU'RE STUCK
• Every step can have a smallest start, a first move so small it's hard to say no to.
• Tap I'm stuck mid-routine to see just that move. Stopping after is allowed.
• No smallest start yet? Next Cue offers a generic one and lets you save your own for next time.
• Drift away mid-step and one gentle check-in offers the smallest start instead of a nag.

START WITH ONE SMALL STEP
• Today leads with the routine that fits right now and names its first step, plus an even smaller way in.
• Reminders name that first step, with a 10-minute snooze when now isn't the moment.
• Templates for mornings, starting work, a task you've been dreading, and evenings come with smallest starts already filled in.

ONE STEP AT A TIME
• Each step fills the screen, with what comes next shown quietly below.
• Tapped Done too fast? Undo it. Can't do a step yet? Move it to the end.
• An optional step timer counts up beside your rough estimate. It never counts down.
• The current step and a Done button stay on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

MAKE ROOM FOR REAL LIFE
• Mark steps optional and run a short version on low-energy days. It still counts.
• Pause any time and pick up exactly where you left off.
• No streaks, no guilt. Yesterday's unfinished run quietly clears for a fresh start.
