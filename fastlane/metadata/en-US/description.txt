A clearer next step for the routines that shape your day.

Next Cue is a routine coach and daily planner for people who want a little more structure. Turn a big intention into small, visible steps. Start with just the first one, keep the next action in view, and get a gentle check-in if your attention wanders mid-routine.

START WITH ONE SMALL STEP
• Today leads with the routine that fits right now and names its first step.
• See about how long a routine takes and when you'd be done if you started now.
• Reminders name the first step, with a 10-minute snooze when now isn't the moment.

ONE STEP AT A TIME
• Each step fills the screen, with what comes next shown quietly below.
• Tapped Done too fast? Undo it. Can't do a step yet? Move it to the end.
• An optional step timer counts up beside your estimate. It never counts down.
• The current step and a Done button stay on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

MAKE ROOM FOR REAL LIFE
• Mark steps optional and run a short version on low-energy days. It still counts.
• Drift away mid-step and Next Cue checks in once, gently. Mark it done from the notification.
• Pause any time and pick up exactly where you left off.
• No streaks, no guilt. Yesterday's unfinished run quietly clears for a fresh start.

MAKE A ROUTINE
• Start from morning, out-the-door, work-start, or evening templates, or build your own.
• Type steps quickly, reorder them, and set a rough time for each.
• Choose the days and time for each routine's reminder.
