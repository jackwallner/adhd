# Privacy Policy, Next Cue

> How Next Cue handles routine information and purchases.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/adhd/privacy-policy.html

Effective September 22, 2026

## Privacy Policy

Next Cue is a routine planning app. It does not require an account.
Routine information is stored on your device and is not sent to a Next
Cue server.

## Information stored on your device

The routines, steps, reminder preferences, and completion information
you enter are stored in the app on your device. iOS may include app
data in a device backup according to your Apple account settings. Next
Cue does not request health records, contacts, or location for its
routine planning features.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Next Cue schedules reminders and step
check-ins through iOS on your device. They are local notifications,
not messages sent from a Next Cue server.

## Live Activities

While a routine runs, Next Cue can show the current step on your Lock
Screen and in the Dynamic Island. iOS draws this from data on your
device. Next Cue does not use push notifications or send your steps
to a server to do this. You can turn Live Activities off for Next Cue
in the Settings app.

## Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. Next Cue uses RevenueCat to load
purchase options and check whether a purchase is active. RevenueCat
receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information
needed to provide that service. It does not receive your routines or
completion information. See the
[RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Tracking and sharing

Next Cue does not sell personal information or use routine information
for advertising. We do not share routine content with third parties.

## Deleting information

You can remove routine information in the app. Uninstalling Next Cue
removes the app's local data from that device. Purchase records remain
with Apple and RevenueCat as needed to restore purchases and provide
the subscription service.

## Children

Next Cue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. For privacy
questions, email
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com).
