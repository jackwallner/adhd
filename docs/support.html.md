# Support, Next Cue

> Get support for Next Cue.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/adhd/support.html

We're here to help

## Next Cue Support

Email
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com?subject=Next%20Cue%20Support)
and include your device model and iOS version. Please do not include
sensitive health information.

## Restore a purchase

Open Next Cue settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure your
device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original
purchase.

## Reminders are not appearing

Open iOS Settings, choose Notifications, then Next Cue. Confirm
notifications are allowed and check Focus settings for any limits.

## Routine information

Your routine information is stored on your device. Keep an iOS backup
if you want to preserve app data when moving to another device.
